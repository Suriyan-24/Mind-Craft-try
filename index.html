ℹ️ About Mind Craft

Mind Craft is a gamified personal development platform that transforms your daily habits into an RPG-like experience. Level up your real-life skills through consistent practice and achievement.

🎮 How It Works:

  • 📊 Track your daily activities and habits
  • 🎯 Complete missions to gain XP and level up
  • 📈 Watch your stats improve as you build better habits
  • 🏆 Compete with others on the global leaderboard
  • 🤖 Get personalized coaching from your AI companion

📊 Your Stats Explained:

Intelligence: Improved through reading, learning, and problem-solving
Focus: Enhanced by meditation, deep work, and concentration exercises
Stamina: Built through exercise, proper sleep, and healthy nutrition
Strategy: Developed by planning, goal-setting, and strategic thinking
Willpower: Strengthened by discipline, habit formation, and self-control
Mental Clutter: Reduced through organization, mindfulness, and stress management
